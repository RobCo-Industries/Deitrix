Garden plan

Four beds, from the house side:

1. Beans and peas, on the long canes.
2. Onions and carrots, in rows.
3. Squash, two plants only. They spread.
4. Herbs: thyme, mint in a pot, parsley.

Water in the evening, not at noon. The rain barrel fills in two days of steady rain.

Leave the corner by the shed wild for the bees.
