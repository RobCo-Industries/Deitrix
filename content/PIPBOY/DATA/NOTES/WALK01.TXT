Walk to the spring

Left the house after breakfast and took the lane west. The morning was cool and the grass was wet up to the ankle.

The spring runs well this week. I filled both bottles and sat on the flat stone for a while. A heron stood in the shallows and did not move until I stood up.

On the way back I took the footpath over the bridge. One plank is loose near the middle. Bring two nails and the small hammer next time.
